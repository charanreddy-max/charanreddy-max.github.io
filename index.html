Baked small, baked daily

Blue Door started as one oven and a handful of recipes from the family kitchen. Today we still mix every dough by hand and bake in small batches, so what you buy was made that morning.

We use simple ingredients: flour, butter, eggs, and time. If we sell out, we sell out.
